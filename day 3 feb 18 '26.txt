ipv6 compression rules:
1. all zeros on the left are omitted.
	:000a: > :a:
2. subsequent zeros turn into double colon 
	:0000:0000:0000: > ::
3. when there are two subsequnet zeros left to right will be the case.

:a:c::/64
::/0
fe80::a:0:0:f/64
2002:6500:0:3000::/64
::1/128 = loopback ipv6 address
ff00::beef:a00a:aa0:0:0/8

binary	vs	dec	vs	hex

0111		7

/27
FOR IPV4: = (4TH OCTET, 32i)

/100 = 7TH HEXTET, 1ST HEXADECIMAL, 1i

/53 = 4th hextet, 2nd hexadecimal, 8i


ipv6 subnetting:

examples:

1. design an ipv6 networkfor 48 users using the available ipv6 address space of fec0:aabb:fafa:dada::/64
C: 48 users = 6 bits
S: 128 (constant for ipv6) - 6bits = /122 (8th, 3rd, 4i)
I: ipasok si 4i sa 8th hex, 3rd hexadecimal

fec0:aabb:fafa:dada:0000:0000:0000:0000/122 (expanded ipv6 address) 

new answer: fec0:aabb:fafa:dada:0000:0000:0000:0040/122

2. design an ipv6 networkfor 900 users using the available ipv6 address space of 2001:db8::/64
C: 900 = 10 bits
S: 128 - 10 = /118 (8th hextet, 2nd hexadecimal, 4i)
I: ipasok si 4i sa 8th hex, 2nd hexadecimal
2001:0db8:0000:0000:0000:0000:0000:0000:/118 (expanded add)
2001:0db8:0000:0000:0000:0000:0000:0400:/118 (final answer)

Network: 2001:0db8:0000:0000:0000:0000:0000:0400:/118
First valid (+1):
2001:0db8:0000:0000:0000:0000:0000:0401:/118
Last valid: (-1 not):
2001:0db8:0000:0000:0000:0000:0000:07ff:/118
not network: 
2001:0db8:0000:0000:0000:0000:0000:0800:/118


1. design an ipv6 networkfor 98 users using the available ipv6 address space of 2001:db8:a0::/64

S: 128 - 7 = /121 ( 8th hextet, 3rd hexadecimal, 8i 
I: ipasok 8i sa 8th hex, 3rd hexadecimal
